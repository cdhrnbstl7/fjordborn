**Fjordborn 1.14.0: the wilds bite back**

- **Many more enemies:** something new turns up every 25 to 50 seconds, and the "Enemies appear" setting is gone.
- **Sunmeads by day:**
  - **Cinderhorn Ogre:** a fat, horned brute with a burning club. Its fire slam leaves you burning.
  - **Gilded Centipede:** its poison bite keeps hurting after it lands.
  - **Sunscale Stalker:** a red-crested lizard that leaps at you.
  - **Goblin Raiders:** hunt in packs.
  - **Hobgoblin Brute:** wears a spiked gauntlet and has a wide cleave.
- **Sunmeads by night:**
  - **Redmane Ravager:** steel-clawed and pounces.
  - **Cinder Hound:** a red hound that pounces.
  - **Hollow Strawman**
  - **Crimson Revenant:** a rare armoured knight with a greatsword.
- **Rare roaming giant:** the Ember Colossus, a cracked, burning giant with a huge axe.
- **Hoarfells:**
  - **Rimecore Golem:** raises ice spikes around itself.
  - **Frostcoil Serpent:** its frost breath slows you.
  - **Glacier Arachnid:** spits web that roots you in place.
  - **Chained Brute:** a rare brute that swings iron balls on chains and knocks you back.
- **Telegraphed attacks:** big attacks show a warning ring first, so you can dodge them.
- **Afflictions:** burning, poison, frost slow and webbing, each with a warning.
- **New drops:** Gilded Chitin, Hard Scales and Frostglass. The rare monsters also drop gold and Sea Garnets.

**Fjordborn 1.12.0: launcher, ships, ore veins and a castle-galleon**

- **New launcher:** the desktop game now opens to a launcher with news, your version and a Play button. Updates download there with a progress bar, then the Update button installs them and restarts.
- **Shipwright Dock:** build it on the shore facing the sea, then build ships there: the small Faering, the Karve and the great Drakkar. Ships launch off the end of the dock. Each one has a hold: open your bag while aboard to store things. Everything for the dock and the Faering comes from the Sunmeads.
- **Smelting:** a Charcoal Kiln turns wood into charcoal, and a Smelter turns ore and charcoal into ingots. The Forge now shapes ingots into gear.
- **Ore in every land:** tin in the Strand, copper in the Sunmeads, ember in the Gloamwood and rime ore in the Hoarfells. Deposits are much bigger, half sunk into the earth, with real veins of metal, and give plenty of ore and stone. Copper and tin make bronze, for a Bronze Pick and a Bronze Axe.
- **The Drowned Colossus rebuilt** as a castle-galleon with stone towers, turrets and spires, tall masts with torn sails and rigging, ivy, and candlelit windows. The holds have rotting timber, hull ribs, chandeliers, flooded cabins, light through broken decks and a drowned salon full of gold. Chests on the terraces and in the holds hold gold, Sea Garnets and rare materials.
- **Unlock as you find:** recipes and buildings appear as you first pick up what they need, with a notice telling you what is new.
- **Quick bar:** eight slots for tools, weapons, food and anything usable. It fills as you craft, and the bag has + Bar buttons to change it. Food on the bar shows its count and is eaten with a tap.
- **Base lighting:** Lantern Post and Candle Stand, and wall torches now light the room. The desktop game shows up to six lights at once.
- **Sound:** relaxing music with silences between pieces, plus wind, surf and birds. Villagers murmur when they talk. There are volume sliders for master, music, effects, ambience and voices.
- **Camera settings:** look sensitivity, field of view and invert up/down. You can now look up at the sky.
- **Realistic water and sky:** clear shallows and dark depths, layered ripples, sky reflections, sun glitter and thin foam. The sky has proper haze and sun glow, and at night stars, the Milky Way and a lit moon.
- **Movement:** jump onto boulders and stand on them. Swimming is now a breaststroke, and you tread water when still.
- **Ground:** fallen branches look like real branches, and there are 35% fewer loose sticks and stones.
- **Phone:** the sun is dimmer. The home-screen app checks for updates on the title screen.
- **Credits and licences** page. If something goes wrong, the game saves at once.
