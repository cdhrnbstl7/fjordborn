**Valheim-style building and weapon abilities**

- **Building:** walls now have corner logs, six planks, top and bottom beams and a brace; floors are planks on beams and can be walked on even when raised; roofs are thick thatch in three courses on log rafters. All wood has real grain and knots.
- **Workbench:** a heavy log table with a vise, saw, hammer and plank, a back rack of hanging tools under a little roof, and a chopping stump with an axe in it.
- **Inside your hall:** the camera now stops at your own walls and roof, so you can see inside.
- **Wall decorations:** Wall Torch, Shield and Axes, and Hart Trophy. Aim at a wall to hang them; up to three per wall.
- **Weapon abilities (heavy blow):** Club and Iron Mace: Ground Slam. Axes and Iron Battleaxe: Whirlwind. Iron Spear: Lunge. Ember Sword and Torch: Flame Sweep (sets enemies burning). Rime Sword: Frost Wave (slows enemies). Pick: Sunder.
- **New iron weapons** at the Forge from Wreck Iron: Iron Mace, Iron Spear and Iron Battleaxe.
