**A real dodge**

- Space now does a quick dash of about 3 metres in the direction you're holding: forward, back, left, right or diagonal. With no direction held, you dash straight back.
- You keep facing the same way (or keep facing your locked-on target), so you can sidestep around an enemy.
- The character drops into a low crouch and pushes off, instead of tipping over.
- Brief invincibility during the burst, and a very short recovery.
