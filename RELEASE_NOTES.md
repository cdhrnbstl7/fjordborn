**Tutorial and softer sun**

- **Step-by-step tutorial:** the raven now gives one small task at a time (move, gather, craft an axe, fell a birch, craft a mallet, build a fire and a workbench, hunt for hide, get dressed, dodge). Finish a task and the next appears. After the tutorial, the raven goes back to guiding the main saga.
- **Hints on/off:** a new Hints button in Settings hides or shows the raven's counsel.
- **Softer sun:** sunlight is a little gentler and tree leaves no longer shine in it.
- Existing saves skip the tutorial.
