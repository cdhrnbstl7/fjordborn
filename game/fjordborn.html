Fjordborn

Washed ashore on a wild northern isle with nothing but your hands. Gather, craft, raise a hall, and wake the giant that sleeps under the Gloamwood.

Keys
WASD move · Space roll (hold to sprint, or Shift) · F jump · Q or middle mouse lock on · mouse to look · click to strike (keep clicking for a 3-hit combo) · Shift + click heavy blow · right mouse to block, just before a hit to parry · E use · Tab bag · 1–7 tools · M map · P pause
Touch
Left stick moves, push it to the rim to run. Drag anywhere to look around. Hold Block to raise your shield, and tap Leap while blocking to dodge.

Turn your phone sideways

Fjordborn plays in landscape, with the stick under your left thumb and the actions under your right.

Tap this if your phone's rotation lock is on. The game will rotate itself instead.
